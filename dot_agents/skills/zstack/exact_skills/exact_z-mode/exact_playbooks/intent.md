# Intent

For work whose what and why are not settled yet. The output is the intent: what the change is for, the constraints it lives under, and what was dropped. Work it out with the user; bring a v1 early.

1. Write the intent down as a few hypotheses, each with what would confirm or kill it. Brainstorm to widen the options, not to polish them.
2. Settle what an observation can settle with the smallest probe or prototype in scratch: timing, behavior, size, how hard it is to build.
3. A key choice gets two or three options, built in parallel by one agent each in its own scratch dir or worktree, and judged on the evidence.
4. A bench taken while other agents load the host is provisional. Rerun the deciding cells on a quiet host before choosing.
5. Let the prototype revise the intent. When a constraint or the cost of building it outweighs a goal, propose dropping the goal instead of forcing it.
6. Prototypes stay in scratch, and promotion is a rewrite. The synthesis cites the evidence for each claim, says what was kept and what was dropped, and goes into the issue as the settled intent.
